## 🎨 **Design System Integration**

### **Color Coding:**

- 🔵 **Subtotal**: Blue accent (primary color)
- 🟢 **Discounts**: Green accent (success)
- 🟡 **Shipping**: Yellow accent (warning)
- 🔵 **Total**: Blue gradient (prominent)
- 🟠 **Checkout**: Orange gradient (action)

### **Visual Hierarchy:**

1. **Header** - Attention grabbing với gradient
2. **Rows** - Clear separation với card design
3. **Total** - Most prominent với blue gradient
4. **Checkout** - Call-to-action với orange gradient

### **Spacing System:**

- **Consistent padding**: 1.5rem sections
- **Card spacing**: 1rem between rows
- **Internal spacing**: 0.75rem padding
- **Icon spacing**: 0.5rem gaps
